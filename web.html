  • Atmos

    nicktavaf.github.io/mongo-web-demo ↗

    A Yelp-style app for neurodivergent people that rates NYC spaces by their sensory environment: noise, crowds, lighting and accessibility. It's designed to be calm and predictable, with no flashing or sudden animations.

    • UX design
    • Web app
    • MongoDB

  • Fuel-born

    fuel-born.com ↗

    A night-time driving game set in FuelBorn, a world I built for a world-building class where cars are living machines bonded to humans, and a terrorist group sets them on fire. You dodge burning cars and control your speed with your heart rate.

    • Game
    • Heart-rate input
    • JavaScript

    palestinianskilledingaza.com ↗
